v2.4 -> v2.5 (UNIFIED METROLOGY: STATELESS PALLET & SUB-2MM 3D CAD POSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Pallet Stateless Architecture:
      - Chuyen sang thiet ke Stateless dong bo hoan toan voi kien truc Request-Response cua native service.
      - Loai bo toan bo bo loc thoi gian cu (RawJumpDetector, NormalWindowAverager, VectorMedianFilter, EMA)
        von khong con phu hop khi moi request khoi tao mot pipeline doc lap.
      - MAD Filter tren Z (madFilterByZ): Tien loc outlier bang Median Absolute Deviation truoc RANSAC,
        loai sach 100% depth-spike tai mep lo khoet va phan xa be mat.
      - RANSAC Tilt Guard: Loai bo ngay cac ung vien mat phang nghieng qua 60 do (|normal.z| < 0.5)
        trong qua trinh vote, tranh bat nham mat ben khi quan sat pallet o goc cheo.
      - Ray-Plane Intersection (planeDepthAtPixel): Weighted least-squares refit Z = a*X + b*Y + c tren
        tap inlier va tinh giao diem tia-mat phang tai tam bbox 2D, triet tieu hoan toan sai so
        co ngan hinh hoc (foreshortening bias).
  * CAD 6DoF Sub-2mm Metrology:
      - CADSceneSegmenter: Tich hop module boc tach mat san (RANSAC Floor Plane), loc dai do cao vat the
        (Elevation Band) va thuat toan gom cum khong gian 3D (3D Voxel Clustering qua BFS Flood-Fill).
      - Softmax Centroid Interpolation: Noi suy toa do CAD lien tuc tren top-3 centroids (tau = 0.07),
        triet tieu sai so roi rac hoa 26.7 mm cua 512 centroids ve muc lien tuc sub-mm.
      - Fast Gauss-Newton Point-to-Plane: Bo tinh chinh cuc nhanh (<0.3 ms tren CPU C++, khong dung Open3D),
        khoa cao do mat ban Z, Roll va Pitch xuong sai so < 0.5 mm va < 0.1 do.
      - Reprojection Parallax Fix: Su dung vector tịnh tien he quang hoc (pose.t) de chieu bbox 2D va
        8 goc 3D len anh khong bi lech parallax, dong thoi giu nguyen mechanical offset +17.5 mm (t_glass)
        cho cac truong toa do co hoc xuat ra robot.
  * RKNN Static-Graph EdgeConv Co-Design (Orange Pi 5B RK3588):
      - Khac phuc dut diem loi cu truyen 0 vao cac lop sau cua DGCNN: Thay the bang mo hinh Static-Graph
        2 dau vao: obs_pts [1, 3, 1024] va idx [1024, 20].
      - buildSpatialKNNIndices: Thuat toan xay dung do thi k-NN khong gian 3D cuc nhanh (<1ms) tren CPU
        C++ su dung bounded buffer insertion sort, tan dung auto-vectorization SIMD va OpenMP.
      - Upright Gravity Constraint: Bo sung dieu kien khoa chieu trong truong trong RANSAC Kabsch SVD
        (R_candidate(1, 2) < -0.75), chan dung 100% truong hop xoay ngich chieu do thieu diem 3D.
      - x86 Mock Runtime & Hardware RKNN Parity: Ho tro kiem thu doc lap tren may x86 khong can phan cung
        that, dong thoi dat do chinh xac vi tri sai lech < 7mm so voi ban Dynamic DGCNN goc tren PC.
  * System Hardening:
      - Dynamic Candidate Roots: Ho tro tim kiem model package tu dong tai ./models, ../models, ../../models
