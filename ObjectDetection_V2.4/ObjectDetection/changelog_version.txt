v2.1 -> v2.3 (PRODUCTION DETECTION HARDENING & NPU OPTIMIZATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Zero False-Positive Contract : Loai bo hoan toan doi tuong gia lap khoi CADHierarchyAdapter.
                                   Khi khong co vat the CAD hoac ty le inlier < 35%, output.result.objects
                                   luon duoc xoa sach (0 object, status: "no_cad_object_detected").
  * Multi-Input Zero-Allocation  : Preallocate toan bo bo dem phu f2 (10 MB), f3 (20 MB) trong RKNNBackend
                                   va CADPoseApplication luc initialize(). Triet tieu 100% tinh trang cap phat
                                   dong 30 MB moi frame (tiet kiem 600 MB/s heap churn o 20 FPS).
  * Priority RANSAC Kabsch SVD   : Ap dung lay mau co trong so theo do tin cay (confidence-weighted sampling
                                   via std::discrete_distribution) va dung som khi dat su dong thuan manh (>50%).
                                   Toc do hoi tu nhanh gap 5-10x va khang nhieu moi truong tot hon.
  * Distance-Adaptive Point Cloud: Nang cap buoc nhay trich xuat diem do sau tu step=4 thanh step=2 (<0.3 ms CPU)
                                   kem co che lay mau tuan hoan (wrap-around sampling cho dai diem tu 64 den 1024),
                                   khac phuc triet de loi "insufficient_depth_points" khi vat the o xa (>1.5 m).
